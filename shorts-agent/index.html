Private creator tool

shorts-agent

A private, single-user desktop tool that publishes my own original short-form comedy series to my own YouTube channel.

본인 오리지널 숏폼 코미디를 본인 유튜브 채널에 발행하기 위한 1인 전용 도구입니다.

The series

"Po-daeri" is an original character I created: a white Pomeranian working as an assistant manager at a small Korean company, who monologues about everyday office stress. Every episode is scripted and approved by me before production.

What the tool does

  1. Assembles my finished episode (my script, my original character visuals, voice-over, subtitles, sound effects).
  2. Uploads it to my channel via the YouTube Data API (videos.insert) with title, description, tags and a scheduled publish time.
  3. Always discloses AI-generated visuals (containsSyntheticMedia) and marks videos as not made for kids.
  4. Checks the authorized channel ID before every upload and refuses to upload to any other channel.

The tool is not available to other users and does not access other users' data.

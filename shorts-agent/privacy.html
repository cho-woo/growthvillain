Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 7, 2026 · About shorts-agent

shorts-agent ("the Tool") is a private desktop tool operated by an individual creator (the "Owner") to upload the Owner's own original short videos to the Owner's own YouTube channel (UCvK3ORWHTuvq0TZgmitw16g). The Tool is not offered to the public and has exactly one user: the Owner.

1. YouTube API Services

The Tool uses YouTube API Services. By using the Tool, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's privacy practices are described in the Google Privacy Policy.

2. Information the Tool accesses

  • The Owner's Google/YouTube authorization (OAuth access and refresh tokens) for the scopes youtube.upload and youtube.readonly.
  • The Owner's channel information (channels.list, mine=true) — used only to confirm the authorized account is the Owner's channel before uploading.
  • Public video metadata (search.list, videos.list: title, channel name, view count, duration, publish date) of publicly available Shorts — used only by the Owner to study general trends such as length and pacing.

The Tool does not access, collect or store personal information of any other YouTube user, and it does not download, store or re-publish other creators' video content.

3. How information is used

  • To upload the Owner's own finished videos to the Owner's own channel with title, description, tags, scheduling and disclosure settings (AI-generated content disclosure is always enabled).
  • To prevent uploads to any channel other than the Owner's.
  • To help the Owner plan original content based on public trend metadata.

4. Storage and security

OAuth tokens and all data are stored only on the Owner's personal computer. They are not uploaded to any server, database or cloud storage operated by the Tool, and no cookies or tracking technologies are used by the Tool.

5. Sharing

No information obtained through YouTube API Services is sold, shared with or disclosed to third parties.

6. Retention and deletion

Public trend metadata is kept locally for reference and refreshed or deleted periodically, and in any case no longer than 30 days unless re-fetched. The Owner can revoke the Tool's access at any time from the Google security settings page (Third-party apps & services), after which locally stored tokens stop working and are deleted.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
